# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

TERX is a technical project. We expect contributors to be professional, direct, and focused on the work.

In practice, this means:

- **Critique code, not people.** "This approach has a race condition" is fine. Personal attacks are not.
- **Be direct but not harsh.** Short, honest feedback is better than vague praise or unnecessary hostility.
- **Assume good intent.** Most people are here to help, even when their PR isn't quite right.
- **Stay on topic.** Keep discussions focused on the technical problem at hand.

## Not Tolerated

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language
- Spam or off-topic promotion
- Deliberate trolling or bad-faith engagement

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported to the maintainers via GitHub. We'll handle issues directly and quietly where possible. Repeat offenders get blocked.
